\textbf{Project definition for my degree:} The MSc Applied Artificial
Intelligence project must involve an investigation of a relevant AI topic that
demonstrates technical depth, meets academic standards, and addresses a
well-defined research question in one or more core AI areas---such as machine
learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision,
generative AI, or AI for industry and robotics. It must also show critical
awareness of the ethical, societal, or deployment implications of AI
technologies. This can be achieved through the design, implementation, and
evaluation of a technical AI solution or through a systematic and critical
literature review that synthesises current research and practice. Regardless
of the approach, students must apply appropriate research methods, engage
critically with existing literature, demonstrate depth of understanding, and
justify the relevance and rigour of their work in line with academic standards.

\textbf{Relationship of this project to the degree definition:} This project
investigates a well-defined complete-outfit recommendation question through
machine learning, deep visual representations and computer vision. It designs,
implements and evaluates a system combining frozen vision--language features,
a supervised compatibility model and constrained candidate search. Technical
rigour is supported by controlled baselines, architectural ablations,
repeated-seed testing and calibration analysis, while the literature review
critically positions the approach against existing outfit-compatibility and
recommendation research. The discussion examines data bias, domain shift,
privacy, generalisation and deployment limits. The work therefore connects a
technical AI solution with appropriate research methods, critical evaluation
and responsible deployment considerations.
